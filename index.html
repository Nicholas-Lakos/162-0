  1. Choose Classic, Diamond IQ, or Head to Head.
  2. Each round gives you a random MLB franchise and era.
  3. Spin for a player pool, then fill one of nine positions.
  4. You get one skip. Use it when the board is bad.
  5. Complete all nine positions and simulate all 162 games.
  6. Your record is saved to My Stats when you're signed in.
